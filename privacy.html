Privacy Policy

Covers all projects under kranmal.github.io · Last updated 2026-08-24

What these sites are

Free static pages — small tools with no user accounts, no sign-up, and no server-side backend. There is nothing running that could receive or store data about you beyond a standard web server serving files.

Data these sites collect directly

None. Each project fetches public data (market prices, positioning data) directly from your browser and may cache it, along with your light/dark theme choice or portfolio holdings, in your browser's local storage or cookies — purely so the page works better on your next visit. None of this is sent to the site owner; it stays on your device, and clearing your browser's site data removes it.

Hosting

These sites are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, as the hosting provider, may log standard technical request data (e.g. IP address, browser type) for operating and securing its infrastructure. The site owner does not have access to those logs. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Analytics

These sites use GoatCounter for basic visit counts — page views, referrer, browser and screen size. It's cookieless: it doesn't set any tracking cookie and doesn't build a profile of you across visits or sites. See GoatCounter's own privacy policy for exactly what it records.

Advertising

These sites may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites, and — where legally required, including for visitors in the UK and EEA — will ask for your consent before doing so via an on-page consent prompt.

You can learn more about how Google uses data from sites that use its services, and manage your ad personalisation settings, at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and adssettings.google.com.

Third-party data sources

Market data is fetched directly from each project's own data provider (e.g. the CFTC's public API, the CoinGecko API) from your own browser. No account or key is used, and the site owner never sees or stores what you look up.

Children's privacy

These are financial-data tools not directed at children, and do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your jurisdiction).

Changes to this policy

If these sites start collecting data they don't today, or the advertising setup changes materially, this policy will be updated accordingly.
